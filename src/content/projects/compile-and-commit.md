Compile and Commit is my own company, built around a simple idea: the businesses I work with usually need more than one kind of help — a website, some AI-powered automation, a marketing engine that actually reports on itself — and they shouldn't have to coordinate three different vendors to get it.

As founder and CEO, I lead strategy and delivery across all four service verticals: software development, AI automation, digital marketing, and IT consulting.
